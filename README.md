PONS $101 — ROB Lab is an experimental education, research, provenance, and agent-development environment for studying token creation and launch behavior on Robinhood Chain, including launches through Pons v2.

The laboratory combines human-directed experimentation, public blockchain evidence, Discord classroom infrastructure, GitHub reproducibility, mixed-methods research, and specialized AI agents—including agents operated by @hashwarlock—to study what happens from the moment a token-launch hypothesis is registered through deployment, early trading, fee generation, telemetry, after-action review, and public-good accounting.

At the center of the laboratory is:

ROB — Research Observer for Bot-like Activity

• launch discovery speed

FEE EVIDENCE

Research records distinguish:

BASE TRADING FEE

The protocol's ordinary configured trading fee.

CREATOR TAX

A creator-related allocation where supported by the protocol.

OPENING SNIPE TAX

A protocol-defined additional fee associated with an early launch window where supported by the protocol.

CREATOR FEE ENTITLEMENT

The amount attributable to the creator under protocol mechanics.

CREATOR FEE RECEIPT

Assets actually received by the designated creator or treasury wallet.

Observed fee ≠ creator entitlement.

Creator entitlement ≠ treasury receipt.

Treasury receipt ≠ public-good expenditure.
